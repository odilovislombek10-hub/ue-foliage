# Planting standards (web research summary)

Distances to trunk axis / shrub centre.

## Spacing
- Street rows: spacing = 0.6–0.8 × mature crown diameter (small 5–7 m, medium 7–9 m, large 9–12 m). One species per street segment. Formal double rows opposite, not staggered; jitter ≤ ±0.15 m, lean ≤ 3°.
- Courtyard specimens: 8–12 m or crown × 1.0–1.2.
- Groves: trunks 3–5 m apart inside a cluster (0.3–0.5 × crown), odd counts 3/5/7; gaps between clusters 1.5–2 × crown (12–20 m) = open lawn rooms.
- Clearances (SP 42.13330): building wall tree ≥ 5 m (better max(5, 0.5·crown+1)), shrub ≥ 1.5 m; path edge tree 0.7–1 m, shrub 0.5 m; road curb tree 2 m, shrub 1 m; light pole 4 m; playground edge 3–4 m (trees on S/W side for shade), play fall zones free.

## Small / narrow beds by width W
- Facade strip W 1–2 m: no trees; clipped hedge along path edge (column accents every 3–5 m optional).
- Facade strip W 2–4 m: hedge 0.3–0.4 m in from path edge, shrub mass behind; small/columnar trees every 6–8 m only if wall clearance ≥ 3 m.
- Bed between parallel paths W 2–5 m: one tree row on the centreline at 6–8 m; hedge edging; if W ≥ 4 m a shrub drift under every second tree.
- Lawn island in paved plaza (5–15 m): one specimen shade tree off-centre (~1/3) or cluster of 3; low hedge ring 0.5–1 m; ≥ 60 % stays lawn.
- Corner beds: sight triangle nothing > 0.6–0.8 m within 5–10 m of corner; accent at back, drift at front.

## Hedges and shrub masses
- Clipped hedge single row 3 plants/m (Buxus 4–5/m); double staggered rows 0.3–0.4 m apart, 5–7/m. Centreline 0.3–0.5 m in from bed/path edge. Uniform size, NO jitter, follows the edge exactly.
- Shrub masses: large 1–1.5/m², medium 2–4/m², triangular grid ±15 % jitter. Drifts elongated 2.5–4:1 parallel to path/facade, ≥ 5–7 plants per species, interlocking wavy edges, tall at back → low at front.

## Composition
- Per courtyard: 1 dominant tree, 1–2 secondary, 1 accent (≈ 60/30/10 by count). Street: one species per segment. Hedges one species throughout.
- Repeat the same group 2–4 times for rhythm; accents only at axis ends, entrances, corners.
- Dark conifers never scattered among light broadleaf: only own clusters (3–7), dark backdrop/screen, or formal column rhythm.
- Canopy 35–50 % over courtyards; soft area: open lawn 40–60 %, shrub beds 20–35 %; ≥ 1 open sunny lawn room ≥ 150 m² per courtyard; no canopy trees within 5 m of south facades.
