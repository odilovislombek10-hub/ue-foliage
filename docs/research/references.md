# Reference rulebook (from the client's 15 references + 7 screenshots of the bad result)

Palette codes: L large shade 12–21 m (Ø10–14) · P columnar poplar · M medium 6–10 m (Ø5–7) · S small 3–5 m (Ø3–4) · C conifer · B boxwood ball Ø0.7 · E mounded evergreen Ø~1.2 · O oleander Ø~1.0

## Shared principles of all references
Linear elements (paths, facades, streets) get LINES of planting; open spaces get planted EDGES and empty centres; every bed has a hedge or shrub edge; almost no species mixing within one view; canopy reads as MASS (clusters, merged crowns), never scattered dots.

## Bed typology (first match wins)
T1 narrow strip Wmin<1.5 · T2 facade strip (facade ≤3 m, Wmin 1.5–6, e>3) · T3 street verge (road, e>4) · T4 playground edge · T5 plaza lawn island (paving all round, A<400) · T6 parterre/formal shape (U, ring, symmetric, formal rectangle e 1.5–4 in plaza) · T7 corner/triangle/L (acute corner <70° or L, A<300) · T8 small bed between paths (A<80, e<3) · T9 curved band (curved, e>4) · T10 medium courtyard lawn (80–400) · T11 large open lawn (>400).
NO BED MAY END UP EMPTY — minimum treatment is a hedge frame or a shrub group.

## Global offsets
Edge→plant centre: B 0.45, E/O 0.7, S 1.2, M 1.8, L 2.5, C 2.0 m. Facade→trunk: S 3, M 5, L 7 (8 with balconies), P 4, C 5 m. No tree within 4 m of an entrance axis; near facades trees sit on window-pier axes. Trunk ≥1.2 m from path edge; shrubs never overhang paths. Same-species spacing: formal row 0.8–1.0×Ø (L 8–10, M 5–6, S 3.5–4, P 4–6 m); cluster 0.6–0.8×Ø (crowns merge).

## Treatments
- T1: one continuous B hedge on the medial axis (Wmin<0.9 → open lawn). No trees.
- T2 (1.5–3 m): B hedge on path-side edge (0.45 m); optional E band 0.8 m from wall in runs of 5–9 between entrances; no trees. (3–6 m): B hedge + row of S on centreline one per pier bay 6–8 m, single species along the facade (M at 8 m if ≥5 m from facade). 2 m open each side of entrances; hedge returns round strip ends.
- T3: one-species allée following the curb, axis at 0.5×verge width but ≥1.5 m from curb; L 8–10 m or P 5–6 m (P when Wmin<4 or avenue character); same asset, scale ±3 %; optional B/E hedge at back edge. Keep 10 m sight triangles and 3 m each side of crossings open.
- T4: M/L on S and W sides, trunks 2.5–3 m outside the play surface, 7–9 m, one species; only B as shrub (≤0.8 m); NO oleander within 10 m (toxic); no conifers.
- T5 rectilinear A<40: B frame + one M/S at centroid (e>2.5: row of S along long axis 4–5 m). A 40–400: B frame + row of M/L along the long axis, count floor(L/8)+1 with 3 m end margins; >9 m wide → two rows. Rounded: one M at the centroid shifted 15 % along the long axis + ring of E at 0.9 m, 1.5 m inside the edge, 50–70 % of perimeter (or full B ring). Keep 30–50 % lawn centre.
- T6: B hedges tracing all edges (both inner and outer where Wmin≤3); accents at ends/axis/corners; trees only on symmetry points (pairs/quads, one species); inside stays lawn; no random shrubs.
- T7: largest inscribed circle r≥3 → 1 L or 3 M triangle 4–5 m; r 1.5–3 → 1 S/M; E drift wrapping the inside corner 5–11 plants, 2–3 staggered rows at 0.9 m; B along street/path edges; sharp tips (last 2 m before <45°) B only.
- T8: 1 tree (S if A<25 else M) at centroid offset ¼ width away from the main path + arc skirt of 3–7 E at r 1.8–2.5 m on the path side; optional B on the busiest edge; symmetric beds: centred tree + full ring.
- T9: shrub band along the medial axis (E 2 staggered rows 0.9 m, or B 1–2 rows 0.5 m), band 1.0 m narrower than the bed at constant offset; trees only where Wmin≥3 (S at bulges, ≥8 m apart).
- T10: one tree group (1 L + 2 M, or 3–5 M) in one quadrant, crowns at 0.7×Ø; E drifts along 30–50 % of path-facing perimeter 2–3 rows deep; B on edges next to seating/paving; ≥40 % one contiguous open lawn.
- T11: 2–4 perimeter clusters of 3–7 same-species trees (L inside, M at edges, never a grid); 0–1 hero L off-centre at thirds; E drifts of 9–25 at 2–3 path nodes (kidney/lens); ≥50 % one contiguous clearing with inscribed radius ≥8 m.

## Harmony
≤3 tree species per courtyard (dominant ≥60 %, secondary ≤30 %, accent S ≤15 %), ≤2 shrub species + B. Per bed 1 tree species. Per row/allée/hedge exactly one asset. Tone classes D (conifers, E, B), G (mid-green broadleaf), Y (pale yellow-green, sparse): adjacent crowns (gap <3 m) same species or D–G / G–Y only — NEVER D next to Y; Y only as pure rows/clusters (best as street allée). Conifers only in same-species groups ≥3 in dedicated zones (boundary screens, large-lawn corners, T7 widest points), never in facade strips or playgrounds. P only in rows. Same treatment repeats across same-type beds in a courtyard; symmetric beds mirrored. Scale jitter: rows ±3 %, clusters ±12 %, hedges 0–2 %, drifts ±10 %; yaw random, pitch/roll 0 (trees may lean ≤1.5°). Odd counts in informal groups.

## Hedge geometry (B)
Inward offset of the bed polygon by 0.45 m, exact polyline, no noise. Spacing 0.50 m c/c (0.48–0.52, fit count per segment, no end gap). A plant exactly on every corner vertex; resample each segment separately. Formal 1.0–1.2 m wide: 2 rows 0.35 m apart staggered. Uniform scale; taller variant ×1.5 at 0.75 m spacing, never mixed. Stop 1.0 m before openings/entrances; min run 3 m.

## Shrub mass (E, O)
E 0.9 m, O 0.8 m on a hex lattice; drift length 3–6× depth, depth 2–3 rows, long axis parallel to nearest edge/path following its curve (offset edge inward 0.7 m then +0.78 m per row); ends taper to 1 row; tall back (×1.1) low front (×0.85); sizes 5–9 / 11–17 / 19–31, never <5; one species per drift; drifts at tree bases, path nodes, corners, facade bases — never floating mid-lawn. O only in sunny plaza beds / facade strips, never near playgrounds.

## Coverage targets
Courtyard lawns 40–55 % canopy; paved plaza 20–30 %; playground 25–40 % (edges); street verge 60–80 % of length; large open lawn 25–35 % (edges only); facade strips 0–30 %.
Anti-dots: ≥70 % of trees belong to rows (≥3 even) or clusters (≥3 merged); isolated trees only as T8 single / T5 specimen / T11 hero; cluster crowns overlap 10–30 %; ≥8 m gaps between clusters; canopy Ø ≥ 0.35×√A of the bed; post-check: rasterise canopy at 1 m, single-tree blobs ≤30 % of trees.

## Algorithm order per bed
classify → offsets/medial axis → hedges → trees (rows/clusters/specimen with offsets) → drifts → harmony checks → coverage/cluster checks; repair by upsizing or adding cluster members, never by adding lone trees.
